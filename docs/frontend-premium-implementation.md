# Premium frontend implementation

Approved brief: [original plan](source/frontend-premium-plan.md), supplied by the owner on 2026-10-01. Scope: below the completed hero/header; preserve their composition. This is a code-led refinement of the established dark timber identity, not a new brand.

Surface: SK/DE/EN/PL chalet landing page, primarily families comparing whole-property stays on mobile. Main task: inspect the property, understand the adult/child price, choose dates, send a direct inquiry. Quality bar: photography carries the product; Thunder headings, Karla body; amber carries actions and money; no ornamental panels.

Layout families: editorial split intro; asymmetric gallery; full-width photographic pause; three featured amenities plus quiet text inventory; photographic location timeline; large price with shared guest calculator; working calendar and sticky summary; contact-led footer.

Motion: masked headings and decoded photo reveals once; CSS scroll timeline for two photographs with static fallback; geometry-preserving FLIP gallery; direct calendar/guest interaction; reduced motion uses opacity. No per-letter reveal, glow, new cursor effects, counting numbers or magnetic buttons.

Truth: omit unconfirmed distances, bed types, payment conditions and response-time promises. Existing shared pricing is the calculation source. Booking comparison reflects repository rates, not a live Booking quote. Keep existing hero intact; its rating presentation needs separate owner decision. Source photography is owner-selected through this brief; originals remain untouched.

Checkpoints: (0/1) safety, motion and foundation; (2) photo pipeline and editorial sections; (3) calculator, inquiry and contact; (4) gallery/motion polish; (5) visual, interaction, reduced-motion, performance and independent review. Local commits, local preview. Public deployment needs explicit authorization.

Validation: section screenshots SK/DE at 1440, 1280×720, 900, 390 and 320; keyboard and emulated touch; pricing/date/API contracts; production Lighthouse and CPU trace; detector on changed surface. Physical iPhone and live email delivery remain separate checks unless actually performed.
