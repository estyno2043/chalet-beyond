# Chalet Beyond

## Purpose and visitor task

Private chalet accommodation in Veľká Lomnica. The site supports direct stay
inquiries: understand the property, inspect the rooms, check dates, send an
inquiry. Checking dates does not confirm a booking.

Source: existing PLAYBOOK.md and property content. Existing locales are Slovak,
German, English and Polish. The playbook identifies German-speaking visitors as
the main audience; this audience statement was not revalidated in this change.

## Property facts used in the hero

250 m², three bedrooms, up to eight guests, entire private chalet. These are
existing project facts, not newly obtained owner confirmations.
